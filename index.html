Keisō: Diatom Jewellery

Diatoms are single-celled algae that live in shells of glass. Their cell walls have intricate patterns of silica. Keisō turns those patterns into cast pendants and earrings in gold, copper and patinated finishes, packed in a box with the story printed inside.

King's Island Development

An urban design proposal: rows of terraced houses with private gardens, apartment blocks along a tree-lined boulevard with a timber walkway and bus route, and a canal edge to the south.
